How we calculate the estimates

Solar power

Hourly sunlight and air temperature are used to estimate the farm's output:

400 × (irradiance ÷ 1,000) × [1 − 0.004 × (temperature − 25)] × 0.97

The calculation uses a 400 kWp array, a −0.004/°C temperature coefficient and 97% inverter efficiency. Open-Meteo estimates sunlight for a 30° tilt facing north (azimuth 180°).

Wind power

Wind speed measured at 100 m is adjusted to the turbine's 30 m hub height:

hub wind = 100 m wind × (30 ÷ 100)0.14

Below 3 m/s or at/above 25 m/s, output is 0 kW. Between 3 and 10 m/s, output follows the cubic power curve: 50 × (hub wind³ − 3³) ÷ (10³ − 3³). From 10 to below 25 m/s, output is rated at 50 kW.

Daily energy and demand

Today's estimate adds each hourly solar and wind power value. Since each value represents one hour, the sum is an estimate in kWh.

coverage = estimated energy today ÷ 1,500 kWh × 100

The community is 50 homes, with an assumed combined daily demand of 1,500 kWh. The 800 kWh battery is part of the system specification but is not yet simulated in these generation estimates.
